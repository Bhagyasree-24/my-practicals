What is Cyber Safety?

Cyber Safety is the practice of protecting yourself, your data, and your devices from online threats such as hacking, phishing, identity theft, and cyberbullying.

In today's world, where almost everything is connected to the internet, cyber safety is no longer optional — it's essential.
