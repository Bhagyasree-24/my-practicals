Learn from real incidents. Understand how attacks happen and how to prevent them.

Important Cyber Attack Case Studies

India • 2022

AIIMS Delhi Ransomware Attack

One of the biggest healthcare cyberattacks in India.

What Happened: Hackers deployed ransomware that encrypted the entire hospital system, affecting patient records and operations for over 2 weeks.

Impact: Surgeries postponed, OPD services disrupted, sensitive patient data compromised.

Lesson Learned: Regular backups and network segmentation are critical, especially in hospitals.

USA • 2021

Colonial Pipeline Ransomware Attack

Largest fuel pipeline in the United States.

What Happened: DarkSide ransomware group hacked the company through a compromised VPN password.

Impact: Fuel supply disrupted across East Coast, company paid $4.4 million ransom.

Lesson Learned: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) should be mandatory for all remote access.

Global • 2024

MGM Resorts Social Engineering Attack

Sophisticated vishing (voice phishing) attack.

What Happened: Attackers posed as IT helpdesk and tricked employees into giving system access.

Impact: Systems down for 10 days, estimated loss of $100 million.

Lesson Learned: Never share credentials or give remote access over phone calls.

Global • 2020

Twitter Bitcoin Scam

High-profile accounts hacked.

What Happened: Attackers used social engineering on Twitter employees to gain internal access.

Impact: Accounts of Elon Musk, Barack Obama, Bill Gates, etc., were compromised.

Lesson Learned: Even big organizations can fall victim to human-targeted attacks.

Key Takeaway

Most cyber attacks succeed not because of advanced technology, but because of small human mistakes. Stay aware. Stay safe.
