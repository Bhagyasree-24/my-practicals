Master Cyber Safety

Simple, powerful, and practical guidelines to protect yourself in the digital world

🔑

1. Passwords & Authentication

✅ DOs

  • Use 16+ character strong passwords
  • Use a reliable password manager
  • Enable 2FA/MFA on all important accounts

❌ DON'Ts

  • Never reuse the same password
  • Avoid weak passwords like "123456" or "password"
  • Never share your password with anyone
📧

2. Phishing & Social Engineering

Stay Safe By

  • Always hover over links before clicking
  • Never share OTPs or passwords on calls
  • Verify urgent requests directly from official sources
"If it's urgent and asking for money or details — it's probably a scam."

Myth vs Reality

❌ Myth

"I'm not famous, no one will target me."

✅ Reality

Hackers use automated tools. Everyone is a potential target.
