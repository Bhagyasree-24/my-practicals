Common Cyber Scams

Stay one step ahead. Learn how scammers operate and how to protect yourself.

Most Common Scams in 2026

📧

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails, SMS, or calls pretending to be from banks, Amazon, or government to steal your login details.

Most Common Scam
  • ✅ Check URL before clicking
  • ✅ Never share OTP via call
💰

Investment / Crypto Scams

Promises of high returns in cryptocurrency, trading, or fake investment platforms.

  • ✅ Too good to be true = Scam
  • ✅ Verify platform legitimacy
🛒

Fake Online Shopping

Extremely cheap offers on luxury items leading to fake websites that steal card details.

  • ✅ Use trusted platforms only
  • ✅ Check seller ratings
💻

Tech Support Scam

Pop-ups or calls claiming your computer is infected and demanding remote access.

  • ✅ Never allow remote access
  • ✅ Hang up and verify directly
❤️

Romance Scams

Fake profiles build emotional relationships and later ask for money.

  • ✅ Never send money to online partners
🔒

Ransomware

Malicious software that locks your files and demands payment to unlock them.

  • ✅ Always backup important files
  • ✅ Keep antivirus updated

🔥 Remember: If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is a scam.
