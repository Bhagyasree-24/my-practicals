Bhaskaracharya (Bhaskara II)

The Great Indian Mathematician and Astronomer

Early Life

Bhaskaracharya, also known as Bhaskara II, was born in 1114 CE in Bijapur, Karnataka, India. He was one of the greatest mathematicians and astronomers of medieval India. His father Mahesvara was also a mathematician and astronomer, who trained him from a young age.

Major Works

  • Lilavati Focused on arithmetic
  • Bijaganita Algebra
  • Grahaganita Mathematics of planets
  • Goladhyaya Spherical astronomy

Contributions

Bhaskaracharya made significant contributions to algebra, arithmetic, calculus concepts, and astronomy. He worked on the concepts of zero, infinity, negative numbers, and quadratic equations long before modern mathematics developed.

Legacy

His book "Siddhanta Shiromani" became one of the most influential mathematical texts in India. His works influenced generations of mathematicians and scholars.
